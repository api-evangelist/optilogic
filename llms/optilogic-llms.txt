# Optilogic

Supply Chain Design, Optimization, Simulation, and Risk Assessment in One Cloud-Native Platform

## Overview

Optilogic is a supply chain technology company headquartered in Ann Arbor, Michigan. Founded by Don Hicks, the company provides cloud-native tools for network design, optimization, simulation, and risk assessment through its flagship platform, Cosmic Frog.

## Definitions

**Optimization**  
Identifying the best network, flow, or configuration given objectives and constraints.

**Simulation**  
Modeling how the supply chain behaves under different conditions or disruptions.

**Risk Assessment / Opti-Risk Score**  
Comparing scenarios based on resilience, service, cost, and other factors.

**Digital Twin**  
A digital model of the supply chain used for scenario testing.

**Hyperscaling**  
Evaluating large sets of scenarios in parallel using cloud compute.

## What Optilogic Is Not

- Not an ERP, WMS, TMS, or execution system  
- Not a planning, SNOP, or scheduling tool  
- Not on-premise software  
- Not a replacement for transactional systems  
- Not LLamasoft or Coupa  

Optilogic focuses on design, optimization, simulation, and risk assessment.
